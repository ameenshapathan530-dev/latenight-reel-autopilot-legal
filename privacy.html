Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the privately operated LateNight Reel Autopilot application handles information when its operator connects and manages their own Instagram professional accounts.

In brief

LateNight Reel Autopilot is currently a personal-use tool operated by one individual. It is not currently offered as a service for other people to connect their Instagram accounts. The public GitHub Pages site hosting this policy is a static legal-information website; it is not the application's dashboard or database.

1. Who operates the application

LateNight Reel Autopilot is operated by the individual who maintains this project. For privacy questions or data requests, contact ameenshapathan530@gmail.com.

2. Accounts covered

The operator may connect Instagram professional accounts that they personally own or are authorized to manage, including a food-content account and a cat-memes account. The operator controls the content submitted for publishing. Other people are not currently invited to register or connect their Instagram accounts.

3. Information the application may handle

  • Instagram and Facebook authorization information: information returned during Meta's authorization process, account or Page identifiers, and access tokens needed to perform the actions the operator authorizes.
  • Content: video files selected for upload, captions, scheduling details, and related publishing status or error information.
  • Application data: settings and operational records stored by the application, including records in its local database.
  • Technical information: diagnostic or error information generated while the application runs, where available.

The exact information available depends on the Meta permissions granted, the features used, and the application's current configuration.

4. How information is used

Information is used to connect the operator's authorized Instagram accounts, prepare and schedule content, request publishing actions through Meta's APIs, display publishing status, and troubleshoot the application.

5. Where information is stored

The application is designed to run from the operator's own computer. Its local database and selected media files are stored in the application's local project environment. The project has been configured to encrypt stored Meta tokens; the operator should keep the computer, project folder, encryption key, and backups secure. Remote access tools or network settings chosen by the operator may affect who can reach the local dashboard.

The GitHub repository and GitHub Pages website contain public legal-information pages only. Do not submit Instagram credentials, access tokens, private videos, or other personal information through the legal website or its GitHub repository.

6. Sharing and service providers

The application communicates with Meta/Instagram to provide the account and publishing functions the operator requests. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. GitHub hosts this public policy website and repository. The operator may also use local software or services configured on their own computer. This policy does not claim that Meta or any other provider acts on the operator's behalf in every context; their own terms apply to their services.

The operator does not currently provide account access to other users or sell the information handled by this personal-use application.

7. Retention and deletion

Information may remain in the local database, media folders, or backups until the operator deletes it. Retention depends on the operator's use and local backup practices. To ask a question or request help identifying information associated with this application, email ameenshapathan530@gmail.com. See the Data Deletion Instructions for details.

8. Security

The operator takes reasonable steps appropriate to a personal local application, including encrypted storage of Meta access tokens. No computer, network, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. The operator is responsible for protecting device access, project files, credentials, encryption keys, and backups.

9. Children's privacy

This application is not designed for children and is not offered as a public service for users to create accounts.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features, data handling, or use changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published. If the application is later opened to other users, this policy will be reviewed and updated before that change.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact ameenshapathan530@gmail.com.
