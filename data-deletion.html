Data Deletion Instructions

LateNight Reel Autopilot is currently a personal-use application operated by one individual. It does not currently provide accounts for other people. These instructions explain how to contact the operator about deletion of information handled by the application.

Request deletion by email

Email ameenshapathan530@gmail.com with the subject LateNight Reel Autopilot - Data Deletion Request.

In your message, identify the Instagram account or application data concerned and describe what you want deleted. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other login secrets.

What may be covered

Depending on the request and the information present, the operator can identify and delete relevant application records, locally stored media files, and stored authorization information from the application environment. Some information may remain in backups until those backups are removed or overwritten. Information held by Meta or GitHub is controlled by those providers and must be managed through their own account settings and privacy processes.

Disconnecting Instagram

You can also remove the application's access through the relevant Meta/Facebook business integrations or app-and-website settings where the authorization is listed. Revoking access at Meta prevents continued use of that authorization, but it does not by itself guarantee that local copies or records on the operator's computer have been deleted. The operator should separately remove relevant local application data.

Verification and response

The operator may ask for reasonable information to understand and verify a request. Do not email passwords or access tokens. The operator will review the request, explain any limitations, and confirm what action has been taken. This is a manually operated personal-use project; no automated deletion portal is currently provided.

Contact

For deletion questions, email ameenshapathan530@gmail.com.
